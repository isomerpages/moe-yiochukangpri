<p>All students are expected to abide by the school rules and regulations
at all times and conduct themselves in a respectable manner that upholds
the school values.</p>
<p>The school rules and regulations are designed to state clearly the boundaries
of acceptable conduct in school and in society. Enforcement of school rules
and regulations is done with the intention of teaching the students to
uphold certain standards of positive behaviour, in support of learning
and growth in a safe and secure environment.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>1. National Anthem, Pledge and School Song</strong></h4>
<p>1.1. Attendance is compulsory for flag-raising ceremonies.</p>
<p>1.2. Students who are Singapore citizens must sing the National Anthem
and take the Pledge. Students will take the Pledge with the right fist
over the heart.</p>
<p>1.3. All students must stand at attention during the Flag-raising ceremony.</p>
<p>1.4. Students will sing the School Song with pride.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>2. Attendance and Punctuality</strong></h4>
<p><strong>Attendance</strong>
</p>
<p>2.1. All students are expected to attend school during term time. Students
must obtain prior written approval by the Form Teacher, HOD, YH/AYH, Vice
Principal or Principal before leaving the school during curriculum time
and for appropriate reasons only.</p>
<p>2.2. Upon arrival in school, students are not allowed to leave school
without permission. Students will take their lunch in the school canteen
before their Enrichment/Supplementary/Remedial classes, CCA or any school-organised
activities in the afternoon.</p>
<p>2.3. Students attending Enrichment/Supplementary/Remedial classes, CCA
or any school-organised activities are not allowed to leave the school
without informing the teacher-in-charge.</p>
<p>2.4. In case of an emergency when a student has to leave the school or
students who are sick during curriculum time and have to leave school can
only do so if he is accompanied by a parent/guardian. The parent/guardian
will need to sign out at the General Office.</p>
<p>2.5. Students who are absent from school must submit a Medical Certificate
or a letter of excuse from a parent/guardian within 3 school days upon
the students’ return. Students who fail to do so will be marked as ‘Absent
Without Valid Reason’.</p>
<p>2.6. Students who are absent from CCA or Enrichment/Supplementary/Remedial
classes must submit a Medical Certificate or a letter of excuse to the
teacher-in-charge.</p>
<p><strong>Punctuality</strong>
</p>
<p>2.7. All students must be punctual for school, Flag-raising ceremonies
and morning assemblies, lessons, Enrichment/Supplementary/Remedial classes,
CCA and any school-organised activities.</p>
<p>2.8. The music for school morning assembly is played at 7.20 a.m. to signal
the commencement of silent reading in the hall or classroom.</p>
<p>2.9. All students must assemble and be seated in the hall or classroom
for the Flag-raising ceremony by 7.30 a.m.</p>
<p>2.10. Students arriving in the hall or classroom after the 7.30 a.m. bell
has rung will have their attendance marked as ‘Late’.</p>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>3. Conduct during Assembly, in School and Public Places</strong></h4>
<p>3.1. Students are to assemble in their designated places at the assembly
area.</p>
<p>3.2. Students should be reading silently while they are waiting for the
Flag-raising ceremony or assembly programmes to begin.</p>
<p>3.3. Students should proceed to and from the assembly areas, canteen and
classrooms in a quiet and orderly manner as a class.</p>
<p>3.4. Students must queue in an orderly manner when buying food in the
canteen and return all utensils to the designated stalls.</p>
<p>3.5. Students are expected to maintain cleanliness in the school grounds.
Littering is not allowed.</p>
<p>3.6. Students must display respect when interacting with their peers,
teachers and all school staff.</p>
<p>3.7. Students must conduct themselves in a respectful manner that upholds
the school values at all times.</p>
<p>3.8. Students are expected to be attentive, respectful and responsible
learners. There must be no disruptive behaviour in school.</p>
<p>3.9. Students must not steal any item.</p>
<p>3.10. Students must not wilfully destroy school or public property.</p>
<p>3.11. Students are not to exert physical violence on anybody.</p>
<p>3.12. Students are not to extort, buy or sell any items from one another.</p>
<p>3.13. Students are not allowed to have in their possession any weapon.
They are also not allowed to bring any weapon-like item which is intended
to be used to cause harm to others.</p>
<p></p>

<p><strong>Appearance and Hairstyle for Boys</strong>
</p>
<p>4.5. All male students must be clean-shaven at all times. Moustache and
beards are not allowed.</p>
<p>4.6. Fingernails must be short and neat. Nail polish is not allowed.</p>
<p>4.7. Boys should have a student haircut. Boys’ hair should be short and
neat, sloped at the sides and back. The fringe should not touch the eyebrows
and the hair at the sides should not be touching the ears.</p>
<p>4.8. Gel, mousse, hair dye/tint of any kind is not allowed.</p>
<p>4.9. Fanciful hairstyles are not allowed in school. Boys who come to school
with unacceptable hairstyles will be sent home and are expected to report
to school with a student haircut by the next school day.</p>

<p><strong>Jewellery and Accessories</strong>
</p>
<p>4.15. Students should wear a simple watch. The School does not recommend
students to bring smart watches, fitness trackers and other similar electronic
accessories.</p>
<p>4.16. Branded articles, fanciful wrist and ankle bands and jewellery are
not allowed.</p>
<p>4.17. Chains or strings worn around the neck must be long enough to be
hidden under the blouse or shirt.</p>
<p>4.18. Girls can only wear a pair of small ear studs not more than 0.5
cm in diameter.</p>
<p>4.19. All hair clips, hair bands, ribbons and rubber bands used should
be plain black or navy blue. Fanciful hair accessories are not allowed.</p>

<h4><strong>5. Use of Electronic Devices</strong></h4>
<p>5.1. The School does not recommend students to bring mobile phones, tablets,
smart watches, e-readers and other similar electronic devices into the
school premises and on learning journeys.</p>
<p>5.2. If any electronic devices are brought into the school premises or
on learning journeys, the security of these devices are the individual
student’s responsibility. The School will not be responsible for the loss,
theft or damage of any electronic devices.</p>
<p>5.3. The use of the POSB Smart Buddy watch is allowed for students to
make payment at the school canteen and school bookshop. Parents/ Guardians
are advised to enable the ‘Nickname’ feature in the watch to allow for
identification of watch, should it be lost. Parents/ Guardians should disable
the chip immediately if students misplace or lose their watch.</p>
<p>5.4. The use of mobile phones and other electronic devices is not allowed
in the school premises before, during and after curriculum hours unless
a teacher’s permission is obtained. Curriculum hours include recess time,
lunch time, Enrichment/ Supplementary/Remedial classes, CCA or any school-organised
activities in the afternoon.</p>
<p>5.5. The use of electronic devices to capture, store, display and/or transmit
visual, audio or verbal information in the school premises is not allowed
without the explicit consent of the school authority and the persons involved.</p>
<p>5.6. For learning journeys, project work or any other school activities
that require photos or videos to be taken, teachers will select students
to be in charge of the school issued devices that will be used to take
the required photos or videos. The use of personal mobile phones and other
electronic devices is not allowed during the learning journey, other school
activities or on the bus to and from school.</p>
<p>5.7. Any student found to be abusing the use of mobile phones or other
electronic devices will have their devices confiscated by the school authority.
The students’ parent/guardian will have to personally collect the devices
from the school authority at the end of the confiscation period.</p>
<p>5.8. Students involved in capturing, distributing or uploading inappropriate
images or videos of students, parents or staff at any time will have disciplinary
actions taken against them.</p>
