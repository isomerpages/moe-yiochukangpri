<p>YCKPS has embarked on a twinning partnership with Nanhai Foreign Language
School (NFLS) in Guangdong (Foshan), China in 2026. This twinning experience
serves to provide students with extensive exposure to the learning of Chinese
Language and Chinese Culture in an enriched environment; enable students
to develop global awareness and cross-cultural skills; and provide opportunities
for students to learn and interact with peers of another nationality through
the school’s twinning experience at YCKPS’ twinning school in Guangdong
(Foshan), China.</p>
<p></p>
<p>During the learning trip, a group of Primary 5 students visited YCKPS’
twinning school, Nanhai Foreign Language School (NFLS), and were immersed
in the learning of Science through robotics; and culture through traditional
folk art of Chinese Paper Cutting. These lessons were taught in Chinese
Language and the students attended these lessons with their peers from
NFLS. The students also had a chance to interact with their NFLS peers
and shared their impressions of China and their school. The session ended
with a gift-exchange where our YCKPS students gifted their NFLS peers with
our school’s rubric cube, a 75<sup>th</sup> anniversary commemoration keepsake.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Besides the learning in NFLS, the students also learnt through new and
authentic experiences at the Guangdong Museum, Guangzhou Urban Planning
and Exhibition Centre, Nanfeng Ancient Kiln, Cantonese Opera Art Museum
and the Guangzhou Library. It was an immersive, authentic and rich learning
experience for the students.</p>
<p></p>
<p>Back in Singapore, the students continued with the learning reflection
on their trip and also transferred their learning by sharing their learning
experiences with their school mates through a presentation at school assembly.</p>
