<p><strong>Learning for Life Programme (LLP)&nbsp;: CREATE @YCKPS</strong>
</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>CREATE@YCKPS</strong> is designed to provide a vibrant arts education
that extends beyond the technical skillset to cultivate deep appreciation
for the arts as part of the holistic development of students in YCKPS.
&nbsp;CREATE@YCKPS entails:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Character through the Arts:</strong>&nbsp;Using visual and performing
arts as a medium for self-discovery and character-building, developing
resilience, self-discipline and confidence in every student</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Holistic Excellence:</strong>&nbsp;Ensuring that every student
has the opportunity to uncover their aesthetic talents and shine through
the arts, guided by sound values instilled through meaningful arts experiences</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Effective Communication:</strong>&nbsp;Nurturing expressive individuals
who can communicate creatively and articulately through art forms, grounded
in strong social-emotional and life skills</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Appreciation for the Arts:</strong>&nbsp;Developing awareness
and appreciation of diverse art forms and expressions across cultures,
broadening students' perspectives and instilling a sense of identity</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>The Design Approach of CREATE@YCKPS</strong>
</p>
<p>At the heart of CREATE@YCKPS is the adoption of the ‘3E’ framework to
provide ample opportunities for every student to learn and interact through
the arts.</p>
<p></p>
<p><strong>CREATE@YCKPS Experiences</strong>
</p>
<p>CREATE @YCKPS is delivered through a range of purposefully designed experiences
across all levels.</p>
