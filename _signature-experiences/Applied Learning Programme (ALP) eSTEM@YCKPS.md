<h4><strong>Key Focus</strong></h4>
<p>The Applied Learning Programme (ALP) at Yio Chu Kang Primary School, <em>STEM @ YCKPS</em>,
focuses on Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics to provide
students with authentic and meaningful learning experiences.</p>
<p>The programme aims to:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>STEM Literacy and Application</strong>
<br>Develop students’ understanding of STEM concepts and empower them to apply
their learning to solve real-world problems.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Curiosity and Innovation</strong>
<br>Inspire a passion for STEM through opportunities to explore, design and
create solutions to authentic challenges.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Future-Ready Competencies</strong>
<br>Build critical and inventive thinking, collaboration and communication
skills through hands-on and applied learning experiences.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Environmental Awareness (as Context)</strong>
<br>Provide opportunities for students to apply STEM learning in sustainability-related
contexts, fostering awareness of environmental issues and responsible decision-making.</p>
<p></p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Pedagogical Approaches</strong></h4>
<p>STEM @ YCKPS adopts a structured and student-centred approach to make
learning relevant, engaging and accessible:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Tiered Developmental Approach</strong>
<br>A 3-tier model ensures progressive learning for all students:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><em>Tier 1 (Broad-based)</em>: Foundational STEM experiences for all students
to build interest and awareness.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><em>Tier 2 (Enrichment)</em>: Opportunities for students to deepen their
learning through more complex investigations and design tasks.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><em>Tier 3 (Excellence)</em>: Advanced training and mentorship for students
to further develop their strengths and represent the school.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Inquiry- and Problem-Based Learning</strong>
<br>Students explore real-world problems and apply design thinking to develop
and refine solutions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Interdisciplinary Learning</strong>
<br>Integration of Science, Mathematics and ICT enables students to make connections
across disciplines in authentic contexts.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Hands-on and Experiential Learning</strong>
<br>Students actively build, test and improve prototypes, fostering resilience,
creativity and deeper understanding.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
