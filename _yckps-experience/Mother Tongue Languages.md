<h4><strong>Key Focus</strong></h4>
<p>The Mother Tongue Languages Department at YCKPS is dedicated to nurturing
a deep-seated love for languages and cultures. Our core objectives include:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Cultural Connection:</strong> Helping students develop a deeper
connection to their roots through language and traditions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Communication Mastery:</strong> Equipping students with the skills
to use their Mother Tongue effectively in daily life.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Curiosity and Engagement:</strong> Igniting students' curiosity
through interactive and vibrant learning environments.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Bilingual Proficiency:</strong> Building a solid foundation in
the Chinese, Malay, and Tamil languages to foster bilingualism from an
early age.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Pedagogical Approaches</strong></h4>
<p>We utilize diverse and interactive strategies to ensure that language
learning is both meaningful and immersive:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Experiential Learning:</strong> Engaging students in hands-on activities,
such as cultural camps and festive celebrations, to make learning tangible.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Digital Integration:</strong> Using tools like the <strong>Chinese Language Digital Learning Trail (CDLT)</strong> to
provide collaborative and interactive learning experiences focused on listening,
speaking, reading, and writing.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Integrated Reading Programmes:</strong> Encouraging wide reading
through initiatives like the <strong>MTL SOAR Programme</strong>, which
includes teacher-led video series and parent-led reading sessions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Talent Development:</strong> Providing platforms for students to
showcase their linguistic talents through competitions and theatrical exposure.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>MTL School-Based Pedagogy: </strong>P3-6 Oracy Package with SPEARL
&amp; OREOS models and P5-6 Writing Package with FAST model</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p>Our signature programmes are designed to create a pervasive Mother Tongue
culture across all levels:</p>
<p><strong>Whole-Department Initiatives</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Mother Tongue Fortnight:</strong> A dedicated period featuring
vibrant activities designed for the active use of Mother Tongue languages.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>P4 MTL Cultural Camp:</strong> An immersive experience where students
explore their culture through interactive stations, such as traditional
archery and historical artifacts.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Conversational Chinese and Malay (CCM):</strong> A 2-day annual
programme for Primary 3 students to foster strong bonds and intercultural
understanding between different racial backgrounds.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Festive Celebrations:</strong> Annual events for <strong>Chinese New Year</strong>, <strong>Hari Raya</strong>,
and <strong>Deepavali</strong> that include cultural performances, food sharing,
and traditional dress-up days.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Language-Specific Highlights</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Chinese Language:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Digital Learning Trail (CDLT):</strong> A "Let’s Travel with Picture
Books" themed trail for exploring language skills at different stations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Cultural Performance Exposure Scheme (CPES):</strong> Students
attend professional theatre drama, such as <em>The Legend of Sun Wukong</em>,
to deepen their appreciation for the language.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Higher Chinese (HCL):</strong> Implementation of HCL at the Primary
3 &amp; 4 levels to build a stronger linguistic foundation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Reading Programme:</strong> Teachers create special video series
like <em>"Let's See What Our Teachers Are Reading"</em> to recommend quality
books.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Malay Language:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Hari Raya Celebration:</strong> Focused on fostering spiritual
growth and empathy, featuring student performances and educational presentations
about Ramadan.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Higher Malay (HML):</strong> Implementation of HML at the Primary
3 &nbsp;&amp; 4 level to build a stronger linguistic foundation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Tamil Language:</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>School-Based Tamil Competition:</strong> An annual literacy competition
featuring storytelling, spelling bees, characterization, and singing for
P1 to P5 students.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Higher Tamil (HTL):</strong> Implementation of HTL at the Primary
3 &amp; 4 level to build a stronger linguistic foundation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
