<h4><strong>Key Focus</strong></h4>
<p>The YCKPS ICT Department harness education technology (EdTech)&nbsp;to
enrich learning and teaching. Our focus includes:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Digital Literacy &amp; Technological Skills:</strong> Nurturing
students to become future-ready and responsible digital learners.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>21st Century Competencies (21CC):</strong> Using EdTech tools to
cultivate critical thinking, collaboration, communication, and self-directed
learning.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Cyber Wellness:</strong> Prioritizing the safeguarding of students'
well-being in the digital space through positive online behaviour.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Innovation through Play:</strong> Providing dedicated spaces for
purposeful exploration and hands-on creation.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p></p>
<h4><strong>Key Programmes</strong></h4>
<p>Our signature programmes are designed to move students from being passive
consumers of technology to active and responsible creators:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Harnessing EdTech within Curriculum:</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Digital tools are thoughtfully integrated into subjects such as English,
Mathematics, Science, Mother Tongue Languages, and the Programme for Active
Learning (PAL). Through this integration, students develop essential baseline
ICT competencies, digital literacy and technological skills, while also
thinking critically, collaborating effectively, and taking ownership of
their learning.<strong>&nbsp;</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>&nbsp;Harnessing EdTech beyond Curriculum:</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The&nbsp;<em>Code for Fun</em>&nbsp;and <em>AI for Fun</em> programmes,
in collaboration with the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA),
introduce students to coding through interactive and hands-on experiences.</p>
<p>The <em>Thinkers’ Lab </em>is a dedicated space that supports programmes
and activities designed to enhance students’ learning through design and
prototyping, while developing their innovative thinking.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Cyberwellness &amp; Responsible ICT Use:</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>We place strong emphasis on Cyber Wellness to support students’ well-being
in the digital space. Our curriculum helps students harness technology
for positive purposes, maintain a respectful online presence, and become
safe, responsible users of digital tools. These values are reinforced through
engaging assembly talks that relate to real-life online experiences.</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Professional Development for Teachers:</strong>
</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Ongoing ICT training is provided for teachers through workshops that keep
staff updated on the e-Pedagogy in designing lessons, SLS new features
and EdTech Tools, enabling them to integrate these effectively into their
teaching practices.</p>
